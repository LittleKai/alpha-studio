# Event Creative City — Video Prompt Pack

Route thử nghiệm: `/event-creative-city`

## Visual bible

Giữ nguyên đoạn mô tả này trong mọi prompt:

> One connected premium miniature event-creative city at night, soft matte clay 3D render, rounded toy-model forms, refined futuristic event architecture, tilt-shift miniature look, midnight navy environment, luminous cyan and violet architectural glow, restrained warm amber stage lights, brushed metal truss, translucent acrylic light panels, smooth graceful motion, subtle parallax. No text, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.

Bảng màu:

- Midnight navy `#07111F`
- Luminous cyan `#61E8FF`
- Violet `#8B7DFF`
- Electric blue `#2563EB`
- Warm amber `#FFB454`
- White `#F8FAFC`

Thiết lập chung đề xuất: landscape 16:9, 1080p, 5–8 giây mỗi clip, chuyển động chậm, không âm thanh. Dùng cùng một model video cho toàn bộ chuỗi.

## Connector clips

Sau khi render các dive clip, trích frame cuối của clip trước và frame đầu của clip sau. Dùng hai frame đó làm start/end image cho connector tương ứng.

Mẫu prompt chung:

```text
Single continuous cinematic camera move, no cuts. Begin exactly from the supplied start frame. The camera smoothly pulls upward and back out of [DISTRICT A], glides forward across one connected miniature Event Creative City at night, then arrives above [DISTRICT B] and begins to descend, matching the supplied end frame exactly. Preserve the same architecture, miniature scale, lighting direction and camera velocity. Soft matte clay 3D render, rounded premium toy-model forms, midnight navy environment, luminous cyan and violet architectural glow, restrained warm amber stage lights, smooth graceful motion, subtle parallax. No text, no captions, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
```

Các cặp nối:

1. Event Gate → Brief Tower
2. Brief Tower → Concept District
3. Concept District → Storyboard Avenue
4. Storyboard Avenue → Production Workshop
5. Production Workshop → LED Arena
6. LED Arena → Showtime Plaza
